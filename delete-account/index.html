Delete Your Account

You can delete your Runts account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to request deletion, what data is removed, and what happens next.

How to Request Account Deletion

The easiest way to delete your account is directly from within the Runts app:

Delete in the App
1
Open the Runts app and sign in to your account.
2
Tap the profile icon in the top right of the Home screen.
3
Scroll down and tap Settings.
4
Tap Delete Account and confirm your decision.
5
Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted.

If you no longer have access to the app or are unable to sign in, you can also request deletion by emailing us at support@tinkermedia.net with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and the email address associated with your account.

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

Data Action
Account credentials (email, password) Deleted
Profile (name, profile picture, birthdate) Deleted
Listening history and in-progress stories Deleted
Pinned stories and playlists Deleted
Ratings and reactions Deleted
Comments Deleted
Bookmarks Deleted
Followed authors Deleted
Favourited stories Deleted
Downloaded audio files (local device storage) Deleted

What Is Retained

A small amount of data may be retained after deletion for legal, financial, or operational reasons:

Data Reason Retention Period
Anonymised aggregate usage statistics Product improvement No personal link
Transaction and billing records Legal / financial compliance Up to 7 years
Abuse / moderation records (if applicable) Platform safety Up to 2 years

How Long Does Deletion Take?

When you delete your account in the app, your account is deactivated immediately and you are signed out. All personal data is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.

If you submitted a deletion request by email, we will process your request and confirm deletion within 7 business days.

Active Subscriptions

Deleting your Runts account does not automatically cancel an active subscription. To avoid future charges, please cancel your subscription before deleting your account:

  • iOS: Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Runts → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Open the Google Play Store → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Runts → Cancel subscription.
