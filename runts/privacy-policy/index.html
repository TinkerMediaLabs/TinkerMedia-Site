Privacy Policy for Runts: Audio Short Stories

Effective Date: April 15, 2026
Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Tinker Media LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the mobile application Runts (the "App"), available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

This Privacy Policy explains what we collect when you use the App, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how to delete it.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account information

  • Name (display name) and email address
  • Birthdate, used to confirm your age
  • Profile picture, if you add one
  • Sign-in credentials. If you sign up with email, your password is handled by our authentication provider and is not visible to us. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from them.
  • Your chosen favorite genres

B. Content you create and your activity

  • Comments, ratings and reactions
  • Pinned and favorited stories, bookmarks and followed authors
  • Reports you submit and people you block
  • Listening history, how far you have listened in each story, and which stories you have finished. Downloaded audio files are stored only on your device.

C. Device and usage information

  • Device type, operating system and app version
  • IP address
  • How you use the App (for example screens opened and stories played), collected for analytics
  • Crash and error diagnostics
  • A push notification token, if you turn on notifications

D. Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your card or payment details. We receive your subscription status so that we can unlock Premium content.

E. Cookies and similar technologies

The App uses software development kits (SDKs) from the service providers listed below, for the purposes described in this policy. The App does not use advertising trackers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Operate and maintain the App and your account
  • Send account emails (see section 3)
  • Manage subscriptions and unlock Premium content
  • Remember where you stopped and recommend stories you may like
  • Send push notifications you have turned on, such as a new story from an author you follow
  • Keep the community safe: review reports, remove content and restrict accounts that break our Terms
  • Understand how the App is used, find and fix problems, and improve it
  • Prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations

3. Emails We Send

We send account emails only: sign-up verification codes and password reset codes, from noreply@tinkermedia.net. Each is sent in response to a request you make in the App, to the address you entered. We do not send marketing email.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Payments are processed via Apple and Google. Subscription management happens through your app store account. Deleting your Runts account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your app store first.

5. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the App, and only as needed for them to do so:

  • Amazon Web Services: hosting, sign-in (Amazon Cognito), database, file storage and delivery of audio and images, and sending account emails
  • RevenueCat: subscription status and purchase management
  • Sentry: crash and error reporting
  • RudderStack: app usage analytics
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Expo: delivering push notifications
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose it, and payment processing

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a business transfer.

6. Your Comments and Profile

Comments you post, and your display name and profile picture, may be seen by other users. You can edit or delete your own comments. You can block other users and report content or profiles that break our Terms.

7. Data Retention

We keep your account information and activity while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your personal data, as described in section 8. A small amount of data is kept afterwards: transaction and billing records for up to 7 years for legal and financial reasons, and abuse or moderation records for up to 2 years for platform safety. Backups are removed on a rolling schedule, within 30 days.

8. Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account and data at any time in the App: open your profile, tap Account, then Delete Account, and confirm. If you cannot sign in, email admin@tinkermedia.net with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and the email address on your account. Full details of what is deleted and what is kept are at tinkermedia.net/delete-account.

9. Your Rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or restrict how we use it. You can do many of these in the App. For anything else, email admin@tinkermedia.net.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. The optional mature-content section of the App is available only to adults aged 18 or over.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls, but no system is completely secure.

12. International Data Transfers

Your data is processed in the United States and may be processed elsewhere by our service providers. Where the law requires it, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

13. California and EEA Residents

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information. Residents of the European Economic Area have the rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority. We process your information to provide the App you asked for, to meet legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests in keeping the App secure and improving it. To exercise any of these rights, email admin@tinkermedia.net.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. When we make significant changes we will tell you in the App or by email, and we will update the "Last Updated" date above.
