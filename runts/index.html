Short stories you listen to

Runts is a mobile app of short fiction told out loud. Browse by genre or author, pin stories to your playlist, set a sleep timer, and pick up where you left off, whether you are on your commute, walking the dog or winding down for the night.

You need an account to listen. You can sign up with your email address, which we confirm with a one-time code, or sign in with Google or Apple. Some stories are free and a Premium subscription unlocks more.

Questions, problems or feedback? Email admin@tinkermedia.net. To delete your account and data, see how to delete your account.
